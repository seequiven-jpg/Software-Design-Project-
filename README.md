# SmartX E-Commerce Management System

## About
SmartX is a prototype system we built for our client Alwin, who runs a t-shirt business on Shopee. Right now he manages his stock and finances manually, so our system helps him track inventory automatically and reconcile his Shopee payouts with his bank statements without doing everything by hand.

This is for ITS64204 Software Design, Taylor's University, under Dr Hemalatha.

## What the system does
- Inventory Management - track stock, reorder alerts, picking list, demand forecasting
- Financial Reconciliation - match Shopee settlements with bank statements, generate reports

Different users (Alwin, Farah, Warehouse Staff, Admin) see different parts of the system based on their role.
